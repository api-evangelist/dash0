Emissary Ingress is more than just an ingress controller - it’s an API gateway built on Envoy. In this fourth installment of our series on ingress observability with OpenTelemetry, we show how to capture traces, metrics, and logs from Emissary, unify them with the Collector, and make the edge of your cluster fully observable.
