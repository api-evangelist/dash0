Ingress controllers are the front door to your Kubernetes cluster, making their observability critical. This post dives into Contour, built on Envoy and popular with Gateway API adopters. We’ll show how to bring together tracing, logs, and metrics with OpenTelemetry and Dash0, with a ready-to-run demo in the dash0-examples repository.
